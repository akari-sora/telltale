When an ELM327 clone goes silent after a command, the app now asks it to answer (ATI) instead of dropping the link within seconds. If it stays silent, the app can reconnect to the same adapter once by itself: at most twice in ten minutes, in the foreground, never after a fault-code clear. A single-value reading that times out twice in a row rests for a minute. The session log records what happened. Tested with unit tests and a fault-injection rig; not validated on a real vehicle or adapter.
